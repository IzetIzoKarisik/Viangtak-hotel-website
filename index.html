welcome

A riverside classic in the heart of Tak

For decades, The Viang Tak Riverside has welcomed travellers to the banks of the Ping River. Step outside and you are minutes from the riverside promenade, the golden suspension bridge, and the old town's night market.

Inside, you'll find spacious rooms with river views, a lobby made for lingering, and a breakfast buffet that guests keep coming back for — Thai favourites and international classics, cooked fresh every morning.

Kaeng Mahae

A traditional Tak dish — pigeon peas simmered with pork and local greens in a clear, light broth. Rarely found outside the province, and on our buffet every morning, all year round.

Alongside it, the everyday favourites:

  • The international spread

    Eggs cooked to order, breads and pastries, and the classics you would expect.

  • Fruit & salad bar

    Crisp greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber and sweet corn — dressings on the side, built how you like it.

  • Coffee, all morning

    Coffee brewed fresh and topped up until the buffet closes, with hot milk, honey and Ovaltine alongside.
